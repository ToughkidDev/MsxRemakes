How to play

Controls, rules, modes and tips.

Goal

Make the ball run over every rail on the board before PLAY TIME reaches 0.

Miss

The ball explodes if it enters a square where the rail is broken, a hole, or a block with no rail. The stage starts again.

The basics

Push

The blocks between the cursor and the gap, in the same row or column, all move one square. A block with the ball on it moves too.

Warp

When WARPCOUNT reaches 0, yellow marks light up on the frame. A ball that leaves through the frame comes back in on the opposite side.

Stars

Some stages have a star standing on the board. Push a rail under it and let the ball pass. The star changes colour: taken while red it clears the stage, green gives 2000 points, blue adds 30 seconds.

Time

PLAY TIME carries over to the next stage. At 0 you can continue (+60 s). Stopping the ball uses up STOP TIME 200 (international rules: 3 stops of about 5 s).

Modes

A PRACTICE
Three practice stages, then the main game.
B PLAY
Starts at 1-1.
C REGISTER
A 3-letter name and a 4-character password. Note the password shown at game over and enter it next time to carry on from that stage. The first time, enter ---- as the password.

Tips

  1. Read where the ball is heading and join the rails before it gets there.
  2. Short on time? Speed up. Need to think? Stop the ball.
  3. The fewer pushes, the bigger the step bonus.
